# Meeting Notes

Notes that already have owners and due dates.

## What it does

Meeting summaries and action items. Turns a meeting dump into a one-page note with owners, due dates, and a Slack-ready recap.

## When to use

Use this when notes that already have owners and due dates is the job.

## Connectors

Google Docs, Slack

## First task to send

`Turn these meeting notes into a summary with owners, due dates, and a Slack recap. I'll paste the notes next.`
