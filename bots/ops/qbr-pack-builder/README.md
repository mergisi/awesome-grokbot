# QBR Pack Builder

The QBR pack is a draft from notes, not a deck you send live.

## What it does

Drafts a QBR pack from Slack and Drive notes. Stops before send.

## When to use

When the QBR is soon and the notes are scattered.

## Connectors

Slack, Google Drive

## First task to send

`Draft a QBR pack from Slack and Drive notes for the account I name. Wins, risks, asks. Do not send it.`
